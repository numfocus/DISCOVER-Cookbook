# Scent Sensitivity Guidelines

## Overview
Some individuals have asthma, allergies, or chemical sensitivities that can be triggered by strong perfumes or colognes. These reactions can affect health and participation during conferences and events.

## Recommendations
- Include a **scent-free policy** in registration emails and badges.
- Post **signage** reminding attendees to avoid strong scents.
- Encourage presenters and participants to use **unscented personal care products**.
- Provide **designated scent-free areas** if possible.
- Raise awareness in **conference guides or websites** about scent sensitivity.

## Goal
To create an inclusive and safe environment for all attendees, particularly those with respiratory sensitivities or chemical sensitivities.
